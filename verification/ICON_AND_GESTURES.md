# Icon and interaction update

- Launcher artwork is configured in app.config.js; Android resources are generated by Expo prebuild.
- Animated SVG preview is included beside its PNG source in assets.
- Heart viewBox includes space around its stroke; invalid utility icon syntax is corrected.
- Swipe left on artwork or mini-player for next track; right for previous. Short, vertical and multitouch movement do not trigger tracks. Sliders and library scrolling keep their own gestures.
- Tap outside a bottom menu or tap its handle to dismiss. Back still dismisses it.

Validation: npm run check passes UI syntax/imports, 7 library tests, 6 navigation/gesture tests and 426333 DSP assertions.
