Aiplayed v1.3.1 (Android version code 11)

- New purple and coral ribbon play icon, including adaptive and themed Android launcher icons.
- Sound studio opens with a clear, fully visible “Find your colour” card; its edge shading appears only while scrolling.
- Search fades smoothly as it hides and returns, with a full-height field and spacing above songs and folders.
- Distinct header colours and a clear content boundary, with the A–Z rail retained.

Offline personal mixes continue to use the bundled S-SONDO FP32 model. Music, analysis and listening information stay on the phone.

This release is published only after the candidate passes the APK, interaction, recommendation and Android theme gates. Emulator validation does not establish physical-phone heat, battery or indexing performance.

The existing v1.3.0 and v1.2.6 releases remain available.
